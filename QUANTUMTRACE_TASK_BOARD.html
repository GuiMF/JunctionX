Classification guardrails
Offered ≠ selectedClientHello offers do not prove the ServerHello choice. Declared code does not prove runtime execution.
TLS 1.3 ≠ post-quantumAssess key exchange, authentication, and symmetric encryption independently. Encrypted authentication evidence may be unavailable.
Ed25519 is classicalDo not label elliptic-curve signatures PQ-safe. Distinguish draft Kyber from standardized ML-KEM.
Recommendations follow usageML-KEM is for key establishment. ML-DSA / SLH-DSA are for signatures. Platform compatibility still matters.
Quantum risk ≠ all security riskAES-128 needs nuanced guidance. Expiry, weak modes, rotation, and legacy hashes belong to separate findings with context.
Scores need a denominatorUse deduplicated assessed assets, explain weights, and display coverage separately. Unknowns must not make a scan look safer.
